Om meg

I grew up in a home where both my parents work in IT, so my interest started early. It was the security side that really caught my attention: how systems can be attacked, and how to protect them. I'm now in my second year of studying cybersecurity.

So far, operating systems has been my favourite subject. I was surprised by how much happens behind the scenes that I had never thought about, and it has made me even more motivated to understand systems in depth. I'm not quite sure where I'll end up yet, but cloud security appeals to me. Outside of my studies, I take part in CTFs and work on my own small projects when I have the time.

Classmates would probably describe me as helpful and easy to collaborate with. I like sharing what I learn and solving problems together with others. In my free time, I ride my bike and go to the gym, and follow most sports, from cross-country skiing and ski jumping to Formula 1 and rally. I also play some games.
